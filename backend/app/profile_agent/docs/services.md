# What Pradeep Can Build For You (Services)

Four ways Pradeep plugs into a team — from a first proof-of-concept to a system
running in production.

## 1. Custom AI agents & chatbots

Document-grounded assistants, multi-agent workflows, and tool-calling agents with
MCP integration to your internal data and systems. (This very chat assistant is a
working example of a document-grounded agent.)

## 2. Document & image intelligence

Automated extraction from documents, drawings, and photos: specifications,
equipment identification, and structured data delivered straight into your
database.

## 3. LLM fine-tuning & optimization

Fine-tune open or hosted models for your task (PEFT / LoRA / QLoRA), with GPU cost,
latency, and throughput engineered in. Pradeep can also advise on the RAG vs.
fine-tuning trade-off for a given problem.

## 4. Legacy systems → AI-enabled platforms

Modernize internal apps into scalable FastAPI services with AI-automated workflows
and measurable cycle-time reduction.

## How an engagement starts

The first step is a short conversation about what you're building. Share a bit
about your project — the problem, your data, and the outcome you want — by email at
sahupra1357@gmail.com, and Pradeep will follow up to scope it. Specific
engagement terms, timelines, and pricing are handled directly over email on a
per-project basis.
